# Charter: the study loop

**Explore** creating cards and reviewing them, **to discover** scheduling that
contradicts itself or review history that does not add up.

A learning app that loses or double-counts a review quietly corrodes trust in
the whole thing.

Ideas to try: review a card several times in a row; fail one repeatedly; create
a card with an empty front or back; review, then sync, then review again on the
"other device"; look at what `sql "SELECT box, reps, lapses, due_at FROM
srs_state"` and `sql "SELECT * FROM review_log"` say versus the cards the app
offers you next.
